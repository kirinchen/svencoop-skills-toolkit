cs_bloodstrike PVE - Monster Survival (CS economy)

Hold the map together against 10 waves of monsters.
Monsters come from the T-side of this small arena.
Clear a wave to get healed, a cash bonus and a short break.
Wave 5 and wave 10 are boss waves.

BUYING (Counter-Strike rules)
- You start with a knife, a USP and $800. Kills pay $300, wave clears pay a bonus.
- Walk up to a weapon crate in spawn and press E to buy (the HUD shows [BUY ZONE]).
- One pistol + one primary. Buying into a taken slot drops the old gun.

Optional binds (E on a crate already works):
  bind b ".buy"
  bind , ".buyammo1"    (primary ammo)
  bind . ".buyammo2"    (pistol ammo)
Or just say "buy" in chat.
