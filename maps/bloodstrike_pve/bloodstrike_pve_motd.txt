cs_bloodstrike PVE - Monster Survival (CS economy)

Hold the map together against 15 waves of monsters.
Monsters come from the T-side of this small arena.
Every wave lasts 3 minutes. Clear it, then press E on the Next Wave console:
the sooner you press, the bigger the speed bonus for everyone.
Not cleared in time? Leftovers vanish and the next wave gets up to +25% HP/damage.
20 team deaths in total = defeat. Dying loses your guns.
Wave 5 and wave 10 are boss waves.

BUYING (Counter-Strike rules)
- You start with a knife, a USP and $800. Kills pay $300, wave clears pay a bonus.
- Walk up to the Arms Dealer in spawn and press E to buy (the HUD shows [BUY ZONE]).
- One gun per category: [2] pistol/shotgun, [3] SMG, [4] rifle, [5] MG. Buying into a taken
  category drops the old gun. Buy the same gun again to upgrade it (spare mags, then magazine size).
- Kills pay $300, each cleared wave pays a bonus, fast console presses pay more.
- Ammo: pick a category in the Ammo menu, get one magazine, pay rounds x per-round price.
  Kevlar $2600.

Optional binds (E on a crate already works):
  bind b ".buy"
  bind , ".buyammo1"    (primary ammo)
  bind . ".buyammo2"    (pistol ammo)
Or just say "buy" in chat.
