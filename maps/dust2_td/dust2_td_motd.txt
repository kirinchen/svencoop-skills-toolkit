de_dust2 TD - Gold Mine Defence (CS economy)

Two gold mines stand on bomb sites A and B (2000 HP each).
Every standing mine pays everyone $10 per second. Money cap $1,000,000.
Every monster goes for a gold mine; anyone it meets on the way gets hit too.
Both mines destroyed = defeat. Clear wave 15 (the twin gargantuas) = victory.

TOWERS
- Drop a gun (G): it lands and grows into a tower that fires THAT gun.
- The gun is gone; a destroyed tower is gone. No price, no limit.

BUYING (Counter-Strike rules)
- Walk up to the Arms Dealer in spawn and press E to buy.
- Buy the same gun again to upgrade it (spare mags, then magazine size).
