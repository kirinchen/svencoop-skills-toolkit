de_dust2 TD - Gold Mine Defence (rules v2)

Two gold mines (2000 HP each). Each mine pays everyone $10/s, each miner +$10/s.
Monsters hit players first, then towers, then NPCs, then the mines.
Both mines destroyed = defeat. Clear wave 15 = victory.

TOWERS
- Drop a gun (G): it grows into a tower that fires THAT gun.
- Tower HP = the gun's full ammo capacity: upgrade the gun first, then drop it.
- No ammo = no fire, and a red ! appears over it. Only an ammo carrier can refill a tower.

NPCS (Arms Dealer, E -> 8 Hire)
- Guards: 2 per mine, shotguns, strong. Cannot be hired or replaced. They walk back to the mine after a fight.
- Miners: unarmed scientists, fragile, +$10/s each. 2 per mine at start, hire more ($2000, max 5).
- Ammo carrier ($1000): buys up to 1500 rounds per trip at the dealer with YOUR money,
  walks them to the towers, walks back. Far towers refill slower. Small, unarmed, can be killed.

BUYING (Counter-Strike rules)
- Walk up to the Arms Dealer in spawn and press E to buy.
- Buy the same gun again to upgrade it (spare mags, then magazine size).
